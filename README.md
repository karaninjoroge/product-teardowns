## Why This Repository Exists

Product sense is a muscle. It's built through repetition — looking at products with analytical eyes, asking why decisions were made, identifying what's working and what isn't, and forming clear opinions. This repository is my training log.

Each teardown follows the same template. The last two sections — KPIs and My Recommendation — are the most important. Anyone can describe a product. A PM evaluates it and forms a point of view.
